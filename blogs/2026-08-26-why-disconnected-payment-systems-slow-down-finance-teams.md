Disconnected payment systems slow finance teams down because every additional system, bank portal, or manual handoff adds delay, increases the chance of error, and chips away at real-time visibility into cash position. Most finance teams lose track of payments because their systems were never designed to talk to each other in the first place. A wire moves through one process, an ACH through another, a check through a third, and each one lives in its own disconnected system.
