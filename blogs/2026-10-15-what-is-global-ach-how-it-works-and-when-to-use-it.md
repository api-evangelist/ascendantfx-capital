For years, most finance teams have treated international payments as a two-way choice: mail a check, or send a wire. Physical checks are slow and manual. Wires are a reliable digital alternative, but the fees add up and the process doesn’t scale well once you’re paying hundreds of suppliers, contractors, or employees around the world.
