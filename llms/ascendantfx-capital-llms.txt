# AscendantFX Capital (Ascendant)

> Global cross-border payments and foreign exchange platform serving businesses and financial
> institutions. The aPay platform sends domestic and international payments across global ACH,
> wire, and local rails with competitive FX, Payee Intelligence bank-detail validation, FX risk
> management, and Track 360 real-time payment tracking — 130+ currencies, 200+ countries,
> 3,000+ active customers. API and file-upload integration into ERP/finance stacks (NetSuite,
> QuickBooks, Microsoft Dynamics). No public OpenAPI or developer reference is published; API
> access is provisioned through an Ascendant account manager.
